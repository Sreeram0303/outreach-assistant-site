Outreach Assistant: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who runs it

Outreach Assistant is a personal, non-commercial project operated by an individual developer in India. Contact: captainbannu@gmail.com.

Google user data we access

We request a single scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. It is used only to send an email that you have previewed and explicitly confirmed in Telegram. We do not read, search, modify or delete messages in your mailbox, and we cannot do so with this scope.

To send on your behalf, we store an OAuth refresh token for your account, encrypted at rest. You can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

We also store the email address of the Gmail account you connect, to show you which account mail is sent from and to record it on each sent email.

Other data we store

  • Your Telegram user ID (to restrict access to approved users) and your Telegram first name (to address you in the bot).
  • Post text you paste into the bot, and the link to the post if you share one. The link is stored only to recognise a post you have already acted on and to let you reopen it; we never visit it.
  • Details taken from each post: the poster's name, company, the role advertised and the email address to write to. This is information about other people that appears in posts you share.
  • The drafts generated from each post (subject and body) and your edit instructions.
  • A sent log: recipient address, time sent, the Gmail address it was sent from, and the Gmail message and thread IDs, used to prevent duplicate emails and to show your own statistics.
  • The resume files you upload and the text extracted from them, so the right one can be attached and used to write the email.

Service providers

Post text, drafts and the text of the selected resume are processed by a large language model provider (such as Google Gemini or Groq) to generate the email, and technical traces of those calls are stored with an observability provider (Langfuse). Data is stored on our hosting and database providers. Each provider handles data under its own terms; some free-tier LLM services may retain submitted text or use it to improve their models. We do not send Google user data (your Gmail token or messages) to any LLM provider.

What we don't do

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the providers above. Data received from Google APIs is never used to train AI models.

Limited Use

Outreach Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

You can ask us to delete all of your data at any time by emailing the contact address above; we will delete it and revoke the stored Gmail token.
